About

who I am
  • I started programming at 8 years old making games in GameFruit and Stencyl through courses at Robin Academy. Later, I took a web development course, which pushed me to start learning HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I began working with Python and created projects like Textlogpy, a notes app with login/register systems and basic encryption, and PyML.
  • Over time, I expanded into Linux, Android, and XNU/OpenCore development, where I built multiple Hackintosh EFIs and created tools and guides related to custom ROM development on Linux. After learning Android Studio, I started building Android apps such as OpenBeam and DoMagic. I also worked on a fork of AndroidLiquidGlass, where I fixed resource leaks and added new features.
  • I have also experimented with React, where I built Timely as an early project while learning the framework. Currently, I am 14 years old and constantly trying to improve across programming, systems, and software development.
